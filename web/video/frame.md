# Frame — Cognia product footage

Brand truth for this project. Derived from `web/DESIGN.md` ("The Precision Workbench"); when the
two disagree, `web/DESIGN.md` wins and this file is corrected.

## Palette

| Role             | Hex       | Use                                                            |
| ---------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stage (bg)       | `#0C1115` | Every frame's ground. Never pure black.                        |
| Graphite (panel) | `#151B20` | Callout panels, title-card plates.                             |
| Paper (fg)       | `#F3F1EC` | Headlines and callout sentences.                               |
| Stone (muted fg) | `#8E959B` | Mono labels, provenance, secondary lines.                      |
| Stage hairline   | `#2A333A` | Rules, frame borders, the 24px dot lattice.                    |
| Action cyan      | `#35CEDD` | Lines, dots, the index tick. ≤ 5% of a frame. Never body text. |
| Approval amber   | `#D99A3D` | The approval beat only — a real waiting-for-human state.       |

## Type

- **Geist Sans 500** for headlines (title cards 88–112px, tracking −0.025em, line-height 1.08).
- **Geist Sans 400** for callout sentences (34–40px).
- **Geist Mono 400** for index tags, beat labels, paths and provenance (20–24px, uppercase,
  tracking 0.1em).
- Chinese falls back to the system CJK face at the same weights.

## Rules

- Hairline structure over boxes: a callout is a graphite plate with a 2px stage-hairline border
  and a cyan index tick, not a floating card with a shadow.
- The product footage is framed as a bounded work surface with 14px corners and a 2px hairline,
  on the stage ground with the dot lattice at low opacity.
- Motion explains state: camera moves go to where the task is happening; nothing drifts for its
  own sake beyond one slow ambient breath on the lattice.
- No gradients, glows, neon, glass or aurora. The one "light" is the product itself.

# Personal WeChat (iLink) Setup Guide

This guide walks you through connecting a personal WeChat account to cognia-next through the
**iLink gateway**.

---

## 1. Read the Risk Notice

Review these constraints before you start:

- This connector uses the **iLink gateway** (`https://ilinkai.weixin.qq.com`). It is an
  **unofficial** integration path, not an official WeChat bot capability.
- Automating a personal WeChat account may violate WeChat's terms and carries a real
  **account-ban risk**.
- Use a **non-primary** WeChat account. Do not connect an account that has payments, critical
  contacts, or work communication attached to it.
- Because the path is unofficial and gateway-dependent, availability, protocol shape, and limits
  can change without notice.

Use an official connector such as WeCom or Slack when you cannot accept those risks.

---

## 2. Sign In With QR Login

iLink does not have static credentials. Login is completed by scanning a QR code with the WeChat
mobile app. QR login depends on the gateway and is available only in the desktop runtime (Tauri).

1. Open cognia-next and go to **Settings → Connections → Adapters**.
2. Click **Add adapter** and choose **Personal WeChat (iLink)**.
3. Enter a **Display name** for the connector, for example `WeChat side account`.
4. Click **Get login QR**. The dialog shows a QR image.
5. Scan it with **WeChat on your phone** and confirm the login on the phone.
6. The dialog status advances automatically: **waiting for scan → scanned → confirmed**.
7. After confirmation, the gateway returns `bot_token` and `baseurl`. cognia-next persists
   `bot_token` in the OS keyring as `botToken`, and stores `baseurl` in the adapter settings.
8. Click **Create** to finish.

If the QR code expires, click **Get login QR** again to generate a new one.

---

## 3. Verify the Connection

This channel is **reply-only**, so verification must start from the WeChat side:

1. Ask any WeChat contact to send a message to the connected account.
2. Once cognia-next receives that message, it can reply in the same WeChat conversation.
3. Check **Settings → Connections**. The adapter should show as **running**.

If the adapter shows **degraded** or **stopped**:

- `session_expired_rescan` means the gateway session expired. Reopen the adapter dialog and scan a
  fresh QR code.
- Check the **Audit Log** tab under **Connections** for the exact platform or gateway error.

---

## Notes

- **Reply-only**: the bot can only reply to contacts who have already sent it a message. Every reply
  needs the inbound message's `context_token`; without that anchor, no send is possible.
- **No proactive push**: scheduled tasks, workflows, and other outbound-first messages are not
  delivered through this connector.
- **Session expiry**: when the gateway returns the session-expired signal (`ret -14`), the adapter
  degrades and stops polling until you scan a new QR code.
- **Inbound media**: text, image, voice, file, and video messages can be received. Images are
  decrypted and inlined for model processing; voice, file, and video are represented as markers.
- **Outbound media**: current outbound support is plain text only. Image, voice, file, and video
  segments downgrade to text markers because outbound media requires AES-128-ECB encryption plus a
  CDN upload handshake.
- **No rich text or native interaction UI**: iLink renders plain text only. A2UI surfaces degrade
  through their `plainTextMirror`.
- **Numeric actions**: when an A2UI surface registers numeric options for a conversation, a reply
  like `1` is routed as a button callback instead of a normal chat message.
- **Desktop-only**: QR login and long-poll messaging use the gateway through Tauri's HTTP proxy, so
  this connector is desktop-only.

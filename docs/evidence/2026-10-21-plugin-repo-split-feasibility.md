## Problem frame

Feasibility assessment only, no implementation. The question is whether G1 is achievable now and at what cost. The answer depends on Q1 (motivation), which decides between a full split, a plugins-only split, or keeping the monorepo while making plugins independently releasable. Out of frame: actually moving code.

## Problem frame

2026-10-26 full follow-up acceptance: all five CLI findings, all four API/data-boundary findings and the schema conflict must have rejecting/preservation or successful-runtime regressions. Verify complete semantic recovery/options before any append/credential, raw UTF-8 preservation, lifecycle ownership, parallel fail-closed behavior without caller abort, retained samples, canonical redacted quotes and kind-specific schemas. Then freeze final code, run one public task across all three DS modes with all diagnostics and a16384 judge cap, verify no-op resume and owned resources release, and preserve all historical campaigns.

2026-10-26 repair contract: durable atomic snapshots and single-writer journal, frozen-runtime resume, per-call intent before dispatch, preserved interrupted liabilities, structured judge identities with specific schema errors, explicit communication-proxy semantics, separate structural acceptance and deterministic diagnostic sampling. Verify fault injection, no settled-cell replay, no calls after failed admission, abort/resource cleanup, and unchanged original campaign artifacts. Causal quality improvements and scientific calibration require a new experiment; this repair does not reinterpret the original300 grades.

Make the current evaluation capability concrete (G1): identify the distinct Team and Squad owners, trace supported execution paths, exercise relevant regression tests and reproduce material gaps. Success is a reviewable capability matrix and reproducible commands, with quality, elapsed time, tokens/cost and reliability separated. A real-provider performance claim requires completed representative tasks and comparable baselines; headless unit tests alone do not satisfy it. This audit does not authorize deployment or changes to real user squads (C1).

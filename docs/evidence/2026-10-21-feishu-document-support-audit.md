## Problem frame

Produce an implementation-backed capability matrix and concrete gaps for G1. Verify registration, call paths, platform/auth constraints, and focused tests. Follow-up G2 adds product repairs for F1-F4. G3 expands discovery to Google support and remaining cross-provider defects; G4 authorizes their product repairs and G5 extends Google disconnect ownership across independent renderer callers sharing one native host, without real-account operations. Private-document access and certification of real-provider behavior remain outside this local run (C1).

G6 re-enters discovery for a research-only integration proposal. Preserve existing product behavior; document current owners, primary API evidence, alternatives, permission/auth boundaries and future falsifiable acceptance. New providers, test-account access, hosted OAuth services and deployment are not authorized implementation in this phase.

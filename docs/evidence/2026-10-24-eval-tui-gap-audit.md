### Score and cancellation diagnosis

The official test.sh writes reward 1 only when the complete pytest invocation exits zero; any failed check yields reward 0. Five passing checks therefore do not award fractional task credit. Inspection found that gather cancellation already cancels child tasks; the generated exception handler then calls cancel again. With an additional task waiting on the semaphore, gather raises before the two running tasks finish their asynchronous cleanup. The second cancel interrupts their awaited cleanup. A controlled reproducer importing the unchanged generated run_tasks on Python 3.12.13 confirms two completed cleanups for two tasks, but two interrupted cleanups with cancellation count 2 for three tasks at concurrency 2. Evidence: `.cache/terminal-bench-2026-10-24/cancellation-diagnosis.json`. This supports the mechanism behind the original Python 3.13 official failure; it is not another benchmark attempt or a modified solution. The model's own demo used synchronous cleanup prints, whereas the official case awaits during cleanup, exposing the gap.

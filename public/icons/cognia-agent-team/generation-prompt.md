## Core direction

companion. Preserve the long silver-white hair, recognizable bangs, sapphire-blue
eyes, angular cyan-and-navy ear device, segmented cyan technology halo, cyan
diamond core, and calm intelligent identity in every cell.

Use a premium tactical science-fiction anime character-design language with
layered uniforms, asymmetric short coats and capes, utility straps, technical
fabric panels, compact armour plates, restrained illuminated devices, and clear
class silhouettes. Produce original designs only; do not copy any existing game
character, faction insignia, costume, weapon, logo, or composition.

Use consistent 2.5-head chibi proportions, bust or half-body framing, crisp
deep-navy linework, refined cel shading, and one controlled cyan rim light. Reuse
the same face, hair masses, ear device, halo, diamond core, and base navy-white
uniform. Differentiate roles through outerwear, expression, pose, and one compact
prop or drone.

Install a full Cognia Host on a machine you reach over a saved SSH profile, from its card on the Devices page: inspect the machine, install, repair, pair again or uninstall it, with each step shown as it runs. A Host installed this way gets a "This machine" section and is repaired on its own machine. Behind the `NEXT_PUBLIC_COGNIA_HOST_INSTALLER` build flag until the real-machine smoke passes; builds without it say the installer is not included.

The built-in Chromium browser is smooth and sharp again. Pages stream at Retina resolution straight from Chromium without a second capture per frame (scrolling went from about 12 to about 60 frames a second), frames reach the pane as soon as they are painted, and your clicks, keys and scrolling are delivered in order instead of racing each other. Wide fixed-width pages are scaled to fit the pane, and the toolbar shows the real zoom; responsive pages are no longer left shrunk by a decorative element that sticks out or a moment of overflow while loading. In the lightweight browser, an embedded frame on a page (such as Google's sign-in helper on the Chrome Web Store) no longer takes over the address bar and the tab.

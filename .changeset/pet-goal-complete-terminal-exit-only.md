Desktop pet: only a goal that is actually finished celebrates and pays the goal-complete XP. A goal that pauses (it needs an approval an unattended run could not get, or its judge failed too many times), is stopped, is interrupted by a new message, or runs out of turns, budget or time no longer counts as finished. A goal that is paused, resumed and then finished is rewarded once.

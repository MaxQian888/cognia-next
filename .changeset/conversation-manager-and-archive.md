Add a Conversations page (`/conversations`) with Active and Archived tabs to sort, filter, search, rename, file, archive, restore and delete conversations across every workspace in bulk.

The archive also gets several fixes and additions:

- It shows its count.
- An archived conversation shows a banner, and sending a message to it restores it, with Undo.
- Pins and folders survive archiving.
- "Search everywhere" stays in the archive.
- New: Empty archive, auto-archive after a chosen number of inactive days, multi-select on the phone, and a rebindable archive shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+Backspace).

Chat right rail (Context Workbench): close the loop between the conversation and the panels beside it. The assistant is now told what happened to its revision proposals — rejecting one, or keeping 2 of its 5 hunks, rides along silently on your next message instead of vanishing locally, so it no longer re-proposes what you just turned down. Staged chat context is no longer artifact-only: a workspace diff and a review comment can each be handed to the conversation as a chip, and the send's edit target is now the first _artifact_ among them rather than whatever was staged first (a staged file used to silently disarm the whole review round trip). The session surface gains conversation metadata and conversation-level comments, filling two rail slots that stood empty while the artifact and project surfaces offered eight panels each. In split view, a tab whose conversation is holding artifacts (or a proposal awaiting review) now says so with a badge, so work produced by the pane the rail is not bound to is no longer invisible. Deleting a conversation now clears its artifacts, versions and open tabs instead of leaving them in local storage until an eviction cap happened to reach them.

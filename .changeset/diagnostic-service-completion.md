Crash reports can now actually reach a diagnostic service, and an operator can triage them. The `/logs` Incidents panel gained a working submit button — its consent checkboxes, description box and lifecycle states previously did nothing — with per-report choices about including a minidump or a current-state screenshot, a receipt showing the support code, and withdraw-consent and remote-delete actions. Desktop packages and uploads natively; mobile uploads the redacted report the crash plugin captured. A new `/logs` Service channel is the triage console: crash groups with status, assignee and regression counts, incident detail with its artifacts and audit trail, raw minidump reads gated behind a per-tenant opt-in, and everything shaped to the operator's role. Settings → Diagnostics is where a service is configured; the identity session lives in the system keychain and automatic submission is off by default. The "Report a problem" dialog can send a report to your own service instead of a public issue tracker, and `cognia crash submit` no longer needs a hand-pasted grant — it mints one from the installation's own key, and gained `cognia crash withdraw`.

Sign in with ChatGPT from the Account Center as a separate account type. Login runs in the native browser flow and can be cancelled, retried, renewed or signed out, and the tokens stay in a native encrypted store that the webview and child processes never see. A signed-in account can drive ordinary chat, feature generation and summaries through the Responses API, or run the Codex app-server with its own isolated history. Existing subscriptions, accounts and defaults are left as they were.

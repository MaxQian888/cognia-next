Surfaces that speak for the whole app now report every conversation, not just the one on screen: the tray, the status bar and the title-bar pill show what is running elsewhere, and a phone gets a "N running in the background" chip that jumps to one. Each conversation also keeps its own composer draft context — @-mentions, staged selections and permission mode survive a switch away and back, and in split view the unfocused pane stops writing into the pane beside it. Background panes now detect streaming artifacts and fold in their subagent trees.

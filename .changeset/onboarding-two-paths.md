Redesign first-run setup around two paths and one continuous picture. The welcome screen now forks: **Set up for me** shows everything setup is about to do on a single screen — the agent setups it found to bring over, the past conversations on this disk, how it will reach a model, and what your first task will be able to use — lets you uncheck any of it, and runs the lot on one press before handing you straight to the first task. Nothing is written until you press that button, and on a machine with nothing on it the list collapses to just connecting a model. **I'll configure it myself** keeps the original four-step sequence with every choice open.

The left-hand step rail is replaced by a panel that draws what is actually happening on your machine: runtimes light up as the scan finds them, a signed-in CLI is drawn differently from one that is merely installed, your transcript count is real, and plan lines tick over as they complete. The panel becomes a header band on phones rather than disappearing. Both paths, and the panel, work on desktop, browser and both mobile modes.

Setup also picks up the brand accents from the Cognia website, so arriving from the site no longer means meeting a screen that shares nothing with it. Along the way, the machine-scan step stopped printing internal ids at you — it now says "Claude Code" where it used to say "claude-code".

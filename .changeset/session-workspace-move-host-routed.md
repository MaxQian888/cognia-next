Moving a conversation to another workspace from a paired phone or browser now asks your Host to move it, so the move uses the Host's folders and shows up on every device, instead of changing only the device you made it on. The Host refuses a move while the conversation is running, and an archived conversation can no longer be moved until you unarchive it. Moving a conversation out of a workspace also takes it out of that workspace's folder.

When a biometric check stops an action, the message now says why in words instead of showing an internal code. For example, "Approval not completed (lockout)." now reads "Approval not completed. Too many attempts. Unlock your phone, then try again." This applies to remote approvals, automation consent, device grants (remote control, agent control, file transfer, Locked Use, terminal access), pausing, resuming and revoking a device, signing out, removing a Host, and exporting a backup, in English and Chinese.

Cogsets and cogpacks: save plugin combinations as cogsets and switch between them from the Plugins toolbar (turning off plugins outside the set, except always-on ones), bind a cogset to a workspace, and share a cogset as a signed `.cogpack` file that pins every plugin to an exact version and installs in one reviewed step.

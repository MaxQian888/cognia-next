Terminal dock layout and accessibility fixes: the dock's resize handle now has a larger, easier-to-grab hit target (including by touch) while keeping its thin visible line, a tab's close button is now reachable by keyboard focus instead of only appearing on hover, and the command-completion popup no longer clips against the top of a short dock — it caps its list to the space above the cursor and scrolls internally. The floating-surface sizes were also consolidated into one place for consistency.

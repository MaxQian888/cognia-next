Conversation folders now stay in sync between a paired phone or browser and the desktop. The desktop's folders appear on paired devices, and creating, renaming, reordering or deleting a folder on a paired device is applied on the desktop and synced back, so a conversation filed into a folder on one device lands in the same folder everywhere. A new folder shows up immediately on the device that made it, and deleting a folder still only unfiles its conversations. Against an older desktop that doesn't sync folders yet, the change is kept on the device that made it, the way it worked before. Looking up a folder's conversations no longer reads every conversation.

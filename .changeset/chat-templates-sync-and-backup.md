Saved chat templates now follow the account instead of the machine. They sync to paired phones over the companion protocol, so a template written on the desktop is offered by the mobile composer's `/` menu (and one deleted there stops being offered), and they ride the portable backup with a Domain transfer row of their own, so they survive a move to a new computer. Bodies are stored as encrypted content at rest, like prompt presets. Editing still happens on the host: a template created on a paired client stays there.

Share a conversation's folders and files as a network drive: from the Files panel, give someone a revocable WebDAV or SFTP sign-in for chosen paths, with download and write permissions, an expiry within your file-sharing policy, and steps for Finder, Windows, rclone and OpenSSH. Settings → Connectivity → Network drive turns the host's listener on (this computer, the local network or a tunnel), configures an optional SFTPGo provider, and lists every live share with suspend, resume, revoke, new password and an activity log.

Conversation sidebar: right-click a conversation for the same menu as its "⋯" button, now with mark read/unread, branch conversation, copy link, and export and share. Rows show whether a reply is streaming, waiting for your approval, or failed, and a new "Running" quick filter keeps only the conversations still working, on desktop and mobile. Drag a conversation onto the Pinned header to pin it; while nothing is pinned, a "Drop here to pin" zone appears during the drag. The list is keyboard-navigable as a single tab stop: arrows move, Shift+arrows extend the selection, F2 renames, Delete asks to delete, and Space selects. Pin, archive, move and the other row actions now confirm or report their failure the same way, with undo for archive. Bulk selection gains "Mark read". A conversation handed off to another device lists why its actions are disabled.

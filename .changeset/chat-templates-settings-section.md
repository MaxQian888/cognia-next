Saved chat templates can now be edited and deleted, from a new Chat templates section in Settings.

Saving one used to be a one-way door: a typo in the body was permanent, because nothing in the app ever called the update or delete paths. The section lists every saved template with its parameters, how often it has been used, and whether it carries a conversation setup; editing the body re-derives which parameters the template asks for and shows them live, so the consequence of an edit is visible before saving rather than discovered on the next insert.

Renaming a template deliberately does not invalidate drafts that quoted it — only a change to the message body or its parameters does.

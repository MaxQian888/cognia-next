Web pages open as tabs in the chat's right panel, in Cognia's own Chromium (local dev servers included) once it is installed, and each tab can switch to the lightweight preview for element picking and annotations. A task's pages close when you leave it and reopen when you come back, unless the task is still running, and an agent in a background task works on its own pages without changing the one you are looking at.

Page-by-page flow fixes on desktop and phone. Empty consoles (Bots, Agent Runs, Agent Evaluation, Issues, Workflows, Templates, Chat templates) now offer the next step instead of "select an item" beside an empty list, and controls that cannot work in a browser say so instead of failing: the Creator directory picker, and managed workspace maintenance, which no longer paints a raw "tauri-only command" error. On the phone, screens opened from Me light the Me tab and carry a back arrow that stays inside the app, a blocked screen exits to the tab that opened it, the Squads list no longer runs off the screen, Templates has a title and tells loading, empty and filtered apart, Diagnostics and the Projects header get their padding back, the pet page explains itself with a way out, and "Join shared conversation" moves to the foot of the drawer. Also: the conversation-unavailable page keeps its button beside the message, the status page's uptime figure no longer splits around the decimal point, About cards tighten their header rails, Storage maintenance and the biometric toggles are named for what they do, and internal ADR numbers are gone from user-facing text.

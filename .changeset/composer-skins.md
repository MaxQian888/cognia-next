Composer styles: the chat input box can now be switched between five arrangements from Settings → Conversation, and tuned per-knob (corner radius, padding, send-button shape, monospace input). "Classic" is the default and renders exactly as before — it takes no adjustments by design. "Airy" is roomy and rounded with the status row inside the box; "Dense" is tight and squared with a monospace status line; "Detailed" spells every control out inline with cost and context on their own rail; "Minimal" keeps just the text and the model and folds the rest behind the "⋯" button. No style removes a control — a quieter style only folds it away, and mobile keeps its minimum touch targets whatever is set.

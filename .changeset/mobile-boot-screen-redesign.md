Redesigned mobile boot screen. The Capacitor splash overlay is now a full boot screen rather than a 1.5-second logo: it continues the native splash canvas (same navy, the coin exactly where the launcher icon was, Android 12 hand-over now fades via `launchFadeOutDuration`), adds a breathing halo, two counter-rotating satellite orbits and a spinning accent arc around the mark, and — new — a live timeline of what the phone is actually doing: unlocking the account, loading preferences, waking the native bridge, reading the pairing, reaching the desktop, syncing conversations. Each step ticks with its measured duration and its outcome as a chip (Paired / Standalone / Not paired, Online / Offline / Needs update, Up to date / Will retry, Not needed), a progress bar with a step counter leans into the running step, and once the boot has settled the arc closes into a solid ring with a check badge before the overlay fades. Dismissal is signal-driven — it leaves as soon as the host is linked (or is known not to be), never before 1.2 s and never after 4.5 s, so a stage that fails to report can't strand anyone. While the overlay is up the status and navigation bars are painted to its canvas instead of the app theme. The account / onboarding gates and route transitions on the phone use the same screen (full canvas on a cold boot, a compact in-flow mark inside the themed shell for page loads) instead of the desktop card, and every animation is compositor-only (transform / opacity, no blur, no color-mix) so it stays fluid on Capacitor's iOS 15 / Android 7 WebView floor and collapses cleanly under reduced motion.

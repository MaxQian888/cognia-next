Feishu/Lark as an issue source. A delivery container can bind a Lark tasklist (Task v2) or one table of a Bitable app through a connected Lark account. Tasks and records become local issues, sections become cycles, and edits flow back through the same bidirectional sync engine as GitHub. The add-resource dialog gains the two kinds, a GitHub sync mode with an optional Projects v2 number, and Lark pickers that load tasklists, tables and columns from the chosen account.

The plan review card is readable again and sits above the composer. The plan document gets a real share of the screen instead of collapsing to a sliver. The card has a compact header (status, where the plan came from, step count) and collapses. "Open in side panel" shows the plan at full height, and the decisions stack on narrow panes and phones. Steps edit in place (Enter adds one below, Backspace removes an empty one, Alt+↑/↓ moves one). An edit typed just before approving is always what gets approved, and edits made while another save is in flight are no longer lost. Captured plans are named after the plan's own heading. Refining a plan now updates the plan text and the version the agent is asked to implement.

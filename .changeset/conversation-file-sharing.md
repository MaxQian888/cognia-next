Share a conversation's files: pick files and folders from its Files panel or any conversation share, and they travel end-to-end encrypted in the link, with download and preview in the viewer. Conversation shares now carry their images and honor "include all branches". A file-sharing policy in Settings → Data, plus an organization policy owners and admins can set, limits what may be shared and for how long; shared conversations check the collaborator's `files.share` permission.

Workflow editor: the canvas toolbar is back to a slim row along the bottom of the canvas — it had been stacking its buttons into a tall pillar in the middle of the canvas, covering the nodes being edited. The minimap now sits above the toolbar instead of under its right end, and on a narrow canvas the toolbar scrolls sideways instead of running past the edges. Nodes that need the agent browser, the Pro IDE, the sandbox pool or thread handoff now name that requirement in their "unavailable here" tooltip instead of showing a missing-translation key.

Type `@prompt:` in the composer to find something you sent before, in this conversation or any other, by what it said. Enter references it as a chip; ⌥Enter, or the row's insert button, puts the words back in your message to send again. Only what you typed is used: never the references or attachments that turn carried, and never a message someone else sent in an IM group or a shared conversation. The same words sent more than once show up once.

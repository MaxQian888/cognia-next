Redesigned "New issue" as a full page-style create surface modelled on GitHub's New Issue: a two-column sheet with a document editor on the left (borderless title, Write/Preview tabs over a real markdown renderer, a formatting toolbar, templates, ✨ AI assist — draft/improve/suggest metadata/title/relations — and `#` issue-reference completion) and a metadata sidebar on the right (live board-card preview, status, priority, assignee with "assign to me", labels with colour dots, project, cycle, due-date presets, estimate presets, parent and blocked-by relationships). Drafts autosave per workspace, ⌘↵/Ctrl+Enter submits, and both columns scroll independently on desktop. Cycles management was also rebuilt on the shared UI primitives: a ToggleGroup for cycle vs milestone, Popover+Calendar date pills with start/end dates, and icon-coded status/scope selects.

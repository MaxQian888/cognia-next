Workspace members can now be invited, re-seated, removed and offboarded from the workspace roster, and org owners and admins can change organization roles there too. Maintainers mint one-time invitation tokens for the workspace, org owners and admins for the whole organization. Outstanding invitations can be listed and revoked, and org managers can read the membership audit log in place. Every write goes to the collaboration server followed by a roster refresh, and reasons are recorded in the audit log in any script.

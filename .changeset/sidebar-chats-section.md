Rename Direct Messages to Chats and give the sidebar a conventional top-level "New conversation" button.

The sidebar's first section was called _Direct Messages_ (私信) and drawn with an envelope — a human-messaging metaphor for what is actually your one-to-one conversations with a character. It is now **Chats** (对话), with a conversation-bubble icon, everywhere it appears: the sidebar, the navigation rail, the title bar's Go menu, global search, and the conversation type filter.

Its heading row is gone. Chats is the sidebar's default state, so the search field and the list right below it _are_ that section; the row above them said nothing extra and, sitting directly under "More…", read as one more navigation entry. An open team keeps its row — the team's name is the only thing that explains why the list is filtered — and Chats is drawn as a normal row again whenever it is closed, so it is still the way back out of a team.

The two buttons that used to ride that row now have one fixed home each. **New conversation** heads the whole sidebar, above the navigation, where a chat app conventionally puts it; it names and creates in whichever section is open. The **⋯** list menu sits on the search row, beside the field it acts on. When you switch the list to archived, a chip under the search field says so and takes you back.

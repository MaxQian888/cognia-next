Custom themes now cover every colour the app actually paints with, and both variants are editable. The theme editor grows from 27 tokens to 56 — status colours, the five chart series, workflow node and status colours, the Ultra effort accent, and the brand triple were all live in the UI via Tailwind but unreachable from a theme, so recolouring the shell left charts and workflow nodes on the factory palette. A light/dark switch edits each variant independently (previously one switch re-interpreted a single palette and the second side was overwritten by a derivation on save), a search box filters the token list, and the live preview gained status, chart, and workflow swatches so the new points are visible while you drag them. Aliased tokens keep tracking their source: retint `warning` and the "running" workflow badge follows unless you override it. "Edit a copy" of a built-in or plugin theme now opens that copy in the editor instead of a blank draft, and a plugin's CSS-variable palette is lifted into editable tokens rather than left as an opaque override that painted over your edits. Also fixes three WCAG AA failures in the shipped palette: `--destructive-foreground` was never declared (so `text-destructive-foreground` compiled to nothing at 33 call sites and destructive dialogs used near-black text in dark mode), and light-mode success/info badge text and muted text all sat just under 4.5:1.

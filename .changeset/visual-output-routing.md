Charts and diagrams now have a route. A new built-in `chart-design` skill teaches the exact contract the app's chart renderer enforces, so asking for a trend, a comparison or a breakdown produces a live chart in the artifact dock — themed, hoverable, versioned and exportable — instead of a hand-drawn picture of one. A short routing table now travels with every turn so the assistant picks the right surface for what it is showing (an inline mermaid diagram for structure, a chart artifact for numbers, an A2UI surface when you have to choose something, a canvas for something you will keep editing) and, in an IM thread where there is no artifact dock, stops offering the surfaces that cannot render there.

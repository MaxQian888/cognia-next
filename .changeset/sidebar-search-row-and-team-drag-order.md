Sidebar: the guild rows (Chats + your teams) are one fixed block under the conversation list instead of an accordion that cut itself in two and hoisted the open section over the search field — selecting a team no longer moves the search row or the list. Selecting a guild now scopes the list on **every** grouping axis, not only under "Chats / Team" grouping, so picking a team actually shows that team's conversations; the rows are drawn in the narrow Sheet too, which previously had no way to change scope. Teams can be dragged into any order, with "Move up" / "Move down" in each row's context menu as the keyboard path; the order is remembered per profile and the collapsed icon rail follows it. The search field and the three controls beside it now take the same 32px `rounded-md` box and 13px type as the navigation rows above them, and the search-row menus open downward in the Sheet, where a sideways menu was pushed off the edge of the screen.

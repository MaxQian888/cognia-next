Conversation lists, desktop and phone, rebuilt for speed and reach. The desktop sidebar no longer re-renders every row while a reply streams (unchanged conversations keep their identity), windows any section longer than 200 rows — grouped, dated, foldered or pinned, not just flat search results — and stops loading other workspaces' chats into the merged rail. Times are shown in your own time zone instead of UTC, roll over at your midnight, and "Today" means the same day for the list and the row. Focus and selection rings are no longer clipped, the search row and bulk bar stay pinned while the list scrolls, folder headers stick, titles line up behind one leading icon slot, the scroll hairline under New chat appears, bulk "Move to folder" is one transaction that only offers folders every selected chat can go into, and a new sort or grouping picked from the list's own menu applies at once instead of waiting for the pointer to leave the list. The phone list fits its drawer at any title length, windows its rows, keeps its scroll position, no longer closes the drawer when you swipe a row, and keeps a close control for screen readers without painting a button over New chat. Long-press (or swipe → More) opens an action sheet with rename, pin, mark as read, archive, move to folder, continue on another device and delete — delete now asks first — and failures say so instead of vanishing. Avatar initials skip punctuation, so "Document mobile tab bar #399" reads "DB", not "D#".

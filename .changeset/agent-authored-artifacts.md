The assistant can now create and revise artifacts and Canvas documents directly, instead of relying on a detector to notice a fenced code block at the end of its turn. That means it can name a chart, choose its shape, and revise the one you are already looking at rather than emitting a second copy.

Revisions still follow your review-before-apply setting, deleting an artifact asks first, and the whole surface can be turned off in Settings → Artifacts. Sessions bound to a messaging platform don't get the tools, because there is no artifact dock on the other end.

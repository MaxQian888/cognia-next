Redesigned collapsed issue-board columns as an icon rail: the vertical-text strip is replaced by the status glyph, a count pill and a priority-coloured spine of the column's items. Hovering the rail opens a preview card with the column's label, every issue as a clickable row (with the issue context menu on right-click) and an empty-state hint; long lists scroll and overflow rows expand the column. Collapsed columns stay droppable, tint while a dragged card is over them, and keep quick-add and expand controls. Board columns are now expanded by default — including empty ones — and only collapse when you collapse them explicitly.

Show what an external agent is thinking while it works. Commentary an agent emits alongside its answer — Codex's `commentary` phase, and the raw chain-of-thought some open models expose — now streams into the conversation as its own part instead of being dropped or folded into the reply, and drives live "Agent progress" steps in the run panel and in workflow agent-turn nodes. Reasoning that arrives as raw chain-of-thought is tagged with its provenance, so it can be presented as the unpolished output it is rather than as the model's considered answer. Imported Codex sessions keep their commentary too.

The desktop top bar keeps one shape on every route. It used to redraw itself whenever a chat column came or went — the search pill shrank to an icon + shortcut, the workspace pill disappeared, and the Primary/Secondary Side Bar toggles dropped out — so a team conversation and the rest of the app showed visibly different bars. The bar's own segments are constant now and only the projected column headers vary; the chat header drops its duplicate conversation-list and artifact-dock toggles while it is projected, and the "new artifacts" marker moves onto the bar's Secondary Side Bar toggle so a background artifact still announces itself.

Wallpapers can now rotate, and the switch between them is an actual transition. Appearance → Wallpaper gained a Rotation card: pick a playlist (or leave it empty to mean "everything"), advance on a timer, once a day, or at each launch, in order or shuffled, with a crossfade, dissolve, slide, zoom or Ken Burns transition whose duration and easing you set. Where a transition cannot run in full it says why instead of silently cutting: reduced motion collapses everything to an instant swap, and the readability scrim needs the same layer a crossfade does, so a crossfade under a dim wallpaper plays as a fade and the card tells you so. A blur or opacity drag no longer counts as a wallpaper change, so tuning stays smooth.

The same panel gained an opt-in daily wallpaper. Choose Bing's image of the day, NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day, or your own URL (a direct image, or JSON with a path to the image URL), set how often to check and how many fetched images to keep, and whether each new one is applied automatically. Nothing is fetched until you turn it on, only the source you picked is contacted, and every request is screened against private, loopback and cloud-metadata addresses and pinned to the provider's own origins, so a changed response cannot redirect the download somewhere else. Downloads are size-capped and content-type checked before anything is stored, retention never deletes your own uploads or the wallpaper currently on screen, and failures are reported in the panel with a "Fetch now" button that runs exactly the same path the scheduler does.

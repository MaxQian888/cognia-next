The integrated terminal now animates like the rest of the app instead of popping. The dock slides up from the bottom edge when you open it (and back down on close), tabs fade in/out as they open and close, a freshly-split pane eases into its slot, and the discrete overlays — the command-actions menu, quick-fix, find box, and command-history rail — transition in and out. The tab status dot crossfades between running/exited states. High-frequency surfaces that follow your typing (ghost text, the completion popup, sticky scroll) stay instant so they never flicker, and every animation honors your reduced-motion and motion-speed preferences (Settings → Appearance → Accessibility).

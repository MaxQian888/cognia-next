The terminal's SSH surfaces now say what they can and cannot do on the shell you are actually using. A paired phone or browser reads its session's port forwards through the frames the Host has always answered, instead of retrying a desktop-only RPC every two seconds and printing the transport's refusal code. The forwards rail reaches the mobile terminal, saved launch profiles and host tmux sessions reach its shell picker, and the SSH settings editor, the forwarding editor, the `~/.ssh/config` importer and the device console's connection test each explain why they are inert here rather than looking broken. The remote browser now asks the one RPC that answers on a build without the runtime compiled first, so "this build does not include it" is reachable instead of a bare error code, and a saved SSH host appears in the workflow run-on picker, disabled, with the reason.

Files lists the files agents created, modified or renamed in a conversation's workspace under a Workspace files group: open one in its conversation's Files panel, preview, download, duplicate or use it in chat, on the desktop and on a paired phone (read-only, up to 10 MB); sensitive and generated files are left out, and plain web shows the group disabled (ADR-0223 §8).

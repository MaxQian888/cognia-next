Appearance settings: the detail pane now names the panel you are in (icon, label, description) beside the collapsible live preview, and resets its scroll when you switch panels. The nav is a single tab stop with arrow/Home/End navigation instead of twelve. Accessibility options are grouped into Contrast audit / Vision adjustments / Motion cards, and the animation-speed control is disabled with an explanation while reduce motion is on. Every multi-column layout in the section now sizes off the detail pane itself rather than the browser window, so the panels stop cramming their controls when the pane is narrow.

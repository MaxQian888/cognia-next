Theme packs from the bundled game-world, Arknights, Azur Lane, Genshin, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero and Appearance Demo plugins no longer change your motion-speed accessibility setting, and a plugin theme pack with a motion speed other than slow, normal or fast is now rejected. Those plugins, and the Tactical Mind Dial, now stay off until you turn them on. The Mind Dial panel scrolls on short and phone screens, uses readable text sizes, and supports arrow-key selection. Pet Daily Quests shows its strings correctly, cannot pay out a quest twice, says so when a claim fails, and only runs in the desktop app, where the pet exists. Arknights wallpaper names are all in English, and Neon Noir now sets every theme colour.

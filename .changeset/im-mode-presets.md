IM conversations and bots now have named behaviour modes — Assistant, Delegate, Draft and Silent — instead of a single auto/manual/draft dropdown, with an advanced section for setting autonomy, engagement and authority individually. Each field says which layer its value came from, so a mode forced by an SLA escalation or by assigning the conversation to a person is no longer indistinguishable from one you chose. Delegate is offered only where a team or workflow is bound to carry the background work, rather than being selectable and doing nothing. Picking a mode from the inbox chip, the mobile policy sheet or `/mode` now clears any axis a previous assignment pinned, which would otherwise have silently outranked the choice.

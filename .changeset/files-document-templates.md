Start documents from templates: Files shows a "Start from a template" row on Suggested with a blank page and five built-in templates (guide, to-do list, project tracker, weekly update, meeting notes), and New → From template lists them too. Save any canvas document or artifact as your own template from its menu in Files or the canvas export menu; saved templates can be renamed or deleted and appear in the Template Studio under Document.

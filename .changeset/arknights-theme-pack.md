A new built-in Arknights theme pack, with light and dark operations-deck variants and operator artwork. Theme packs now show a preview image on their card in Settings > Appearance, loaded from the plugin's own assets through the same containment boundary as every other plugin binary. A pack can no longer point `preview` at an arbitrary URL and turn browsing installed themes into a beacon for whoever authored one.

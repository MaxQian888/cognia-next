Converge the chat right-side dock onto a single workbench shell. The dock no longer swaps between the activity-rail workbench and the legacy top-tab chrome when you close an artifact or open the browser — a new session-scoped resource backs the empty state, the embedded browser and the workspace panels. Panel switches now fade in instead of hard-cutting, focus mode zooms in rather than snapping to full screen, and the workbench's narrow/wide buttons actually resize the dock.

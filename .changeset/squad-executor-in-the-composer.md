Hand a conversation to a Squad without leaving it.

The composer's chip now answers one question instead of half of one: **who runs this conversation.** Alongside the presets it lists every Squad you have, and picking one binds the conversation to it. From then on your messages go to that Squad — the lead reads what you wrote and plans from it — and the chip shows the Squad's name where the preset used to be. Pick "Single agent" to go back.

Before this, the only way to work with a team was to leave the conversation for a separate workspace whose chat tab quietly did less: no skills, no memory, no MCP tools, no tool approval, and its history vanished on reload. A Squad turn now runs through the same pipeline as any other turn, and its history is your conversation's history. You also no longer have to `@mention` anyone to start — that requirement belonged to the old tab, not to the Squad.

While a Squad is working, the conversation holds: anything else you type queues as guidance rather than starting a second run over the top of the first. Model and thinking level step aside for a summary while a Squad is bound, because each member brings its own.

The advanced panel is honest about the rest now. Orchestrations this surface cannot run are shown but not selectable, each saying why — "Reach it with @mention", "Reach it with /workflow", or "Not built yet" — instead of silently doing nothing when picked.

The binding lives on the conversation, so it survives a reload, shows up on other devices, and follows a branch.

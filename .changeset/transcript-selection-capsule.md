Selecting text in a conversation now offers Reference, Ask in an aside, Summarize, Explain and Translate. Reference stages exactly the selected passage as a chip that links back to its message, and a selection across several messages cites each of them. Summarize, Explain and Translate answer in a panel next to the selection, without spending a turn. The panel streams, lets you stop or retry, and can copy the result or reference it into the conversation. It also says why when nothing could run: no model is available, the text looks like it holds personal information, or the model returned nothing. Asking in an aside now carries the quote into the aside's composer. Two-character Chinese, Japanese and Korean words count as a selection.

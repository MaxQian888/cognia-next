Redesigned workspace boot screen. The loader that covers app start-up (account read → preferences → interface → workspace) is now one continuous screen across its four owners: it shows a real step timeline — earlier steps ticked with their measured duration, the current one live with a one-line detail, later ones pending — a progress bar captioned "Step n of N" that leans into the running step and snaps forward when a step completes, the runtime + version, and, during the last step, which runtime capabilities (chat, plugins, workflows, integrations, knowledge & agents, desktop tools) have come up. The recovery and onboarding gates keep the same screen up instead of a blank frame, so the boot no longer flickers between two loaders. New motion is compositor-only (transform/opacity) so it stays smooth while the app is busy booting: a rotating accent arc and breathing halo on the brand mark, a slow ambient colour field behind the card, a staggered entrance played once per page load, a highlight sweeping the bar, a check-mark pop on the step that just finished, and a right-hand preview of the app that assembles piece by piece as steps complete. All of it collapses under the reduced-motion preference while the step spinner and the bar position keep reporting status. Later route transitions show only the workspace step and no entrance animation.

The embedded browser's back and forward buttons now grey out when there is nowhere to go, instead of always looking available and doing nothing at the ends of the history. While a page is loading, the reload button becomes a stop button — previously there was no way to halt a slow load short of navigating away. The address-bar history menu now lists where the preview actually arrived rather than what was typed, so a redirect no longer leaves the pre-redirect address in the list.

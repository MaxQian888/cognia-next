# Cognia poster series

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Four portrait concept posters, with PNG originals and WebP distribution copies. Original source artwork remains unchanged.

## 01-workspace

Reference: `assets/readme/workflow-studio.webp`

Use case: ads-marketing. Create ONE finished premium Cognia promotional poster, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio, ideally 1536x2304. This belongs to a coordinated four-poster series. Reference is the Cognia character and illustration language, not a layout to copy. Preserve recognizable white-lilac long hair, blue eyes, cyan halo headset and navy outfit. Art direction: sophisticated anime mascot integrated with tactile porcelain and translucent glass objects, deep midnight navy background #101B30, icy cyan illumination and restrained violet reflections, subtle cinematic depth, clean generous typography space. Poster composition: top 28 percent contains strong typography, middle 60 percent a large purposeful feature illustration, bottom 12 percent contains series number. Wide safe margins, no text over faces. Keep the chibi character charming but thoughtfully composed and beautifully lit; no extra characters unless specified. Unified sans-serif typography, "COGNIA" uppercase at top in white, Chinese headline large bold white on two lines below it, one short smaller Chinese subline. Make all Chinese glyphs exact and clear. No other text, no QR codes, URLs, third-party logos, watermark, fake screenshots, claim badges or decorative microtype. Render the final designed poster including typography.
SCENE: The mascot is in a welcoming three-quarter pose at a futuristic porcelain workspace. A large rounded-square nine-light hub sits in the foreground; a conversation tile, a code pane with abstract strokes, and a finished document orbit it in a graceful ascending arc. One hand offers a luminous document toward the viewer. Feature focus: a unified workspace taking ideas into outputs. Distinct scene, not the workflow-block composition of the reference.
